How to set this up in Gmail:
  1. Click "Copy for Gmail" on your preferred design below.
  2. Open Gmail and click the Gear icon (Settings) → See all settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Signature section and click Create new (or edit your existing one).
  4. Paste directly with Ctrl + V (or Cmd + V) into the signature box.
  5. Set the signature defaults to your new signature and click Save Changes at the bottom!
